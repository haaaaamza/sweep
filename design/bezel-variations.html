Clipboard Bezel — 9 design variations

Mockups for Sweep's ⌘⇧V clipboard history overlay, rendered over a desktop-like backdrop so the HUD translucency reads the way it would on screen. Same data in every mock: a text clipping from Finder, cycling through 9 items.

1Refined HUD — what's implemented now

Header with app + relative time, kind badge, filmstrip, hint line. Balanced information density; the filmstrip gives a sense of place in the stack. Baseline to compare the rest against.

7Compact Pill

The anti-bezel: a one-line capsule near the top of the screen, like a volume HUD. One clipping at a time, dots for position. Nearly invisible footprint, ideal for text-only muscle-memory use; weak for images and long text.

9Grid Picker

A 3×3 board of the 9 most recent clippings, each with a number key. No cycling at all — press the digit (or arrows) and you're done in one keystroke. Fastest selection for power users; drops the "flick through" feel entirely.
